LOCAL EXPERIENCES

DO MORE THAN JUST VISIT

Curated activities, guided walks, wildlife safaris, and artisan masterclasses.

HERITAGE WALK

Old Raipur Heritage Walk

Guided walking tour through 17th-century maths, Maratha-era spice lanes, and copper smith workshops in Gole Bazaar.

WILDLIFE SAFARI

Nandan Van Jungle Safari

Guided early morning tiger, lion, bear, and herbivore safaris across 800 acres of protected forest habitat.

ARTISAN WORKSHOP

Dhokra & Kosa Craft Trails

Masterclass interactions with traditional Bell Metal craftsmen and Kosa silk weavers at Purkhauti Muktangan.

FOOD TRAIL

Chhattisgarhi Culinary Trail

Taste fresh Farra, Chila, Muthia, and Bafauri at historic street stalls accompanied by local food historians.

LAKE EXPERIENCE

Budha Talab Sunset Boating

Evening boat rides on Vivekananda Sarovar followed by musical fountain laser performances.

DAY TRIP
ARCHAEOLOGICAL TRIP

Sirpur & Rajim Excursion

Full-day guided excursion to ancient 5th-8th century brick temples and sacred river confluence points.
