Reasons to Explore Raipur

India's Heritage Gateway - Est. 1875

The Mahant Ghasidas Memorial Museum, founded in 1875, is one of India's oldest museums housing rare coins, tribal artifacts, ancient sculptures, and a window into Chhattisgarh's Kalachuri legacy.

800-Acre Wildlife Wonderland

Nandan Van Zoo and Jungle Safari in Naya Raipur offers one of Central India's most immersive wildlife experiences - tigers, leopards, deer, and exotic birds across 800 acres of lush forest.

Centuries of Living Tribal Traditions

Home to the National Tribal Dance Festival and Aadi Parab, Raipur celebrates Chhattisgarhi folk forms like Panthi dance, Raut Nacha, and Sua dance that have flourished across generations.

The Serene Vivekananda Sarovar

Budha Talab, adorned with a 37-foot statue of Swami Vivekananda, transforms into a magical spectacle every evening with illuminated musical fountains and boat rides.

A Living Open-Air Museum

Purkhauti Muktangan in Naya Raipur is a sprawling cultural park showcasing tribal dwellings, life-size sculptures, Chhattisgarhi art, and the soul of rural India under open skies.
