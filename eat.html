Explore Raipur
RICE BOWL CULINARY DIRECTORY

EAT & DRINK IN RAIPUR

Savor authentic rice-flour delicacies, street food hubs, and traditional Chhattisgarhi feasts.

STREET FOOD FAVOURITE

Farra & Chila Trail

Steamed seasoned rice flour rolls & crispy rice crepes served with spicy garlic-tomato chutney in Gole Bazaar.

TRADITIONAL CUISINE

Aamat & Dubki Kadi

Bamboo shoot vegetable curry & lentil dumpling gravy served alongside fragrant local Jeera Phool rice.

SNACK & APPETIZER

Muthia & Bafauri

Spiced boiled rice flour dumplings & healthy steamed chana dal bites sprinkled with roasted sesame seeds.

HERITAGE SWEETS

Khurmi & Babra

Jaggery wheat fritters & sweet rice pancakes prepared during traditional Pola and Teeja festival celebrations.

LAKEFRONT HANGOUT

Marine Drive Lakefront Cafés

Vibrant open-air cafes offering artisanal coffee, snacks, and sunset lake views along Telibandha promenade.

SUMMER REFRESHMENT

Bore Baasi Experience

Traditional fermented rice meal served cold with green chillies, raw onions, and Chhattisgarhi pickles.
