PRIVACY POLICY

Our commitment to transparency, data protection, and user privacy across Hamara India.

1. Information We Collect

Hamara India collects minimal information necessary to deliver authentic city exploration experiences. This includes voluntary newsletter email subscriptions, community recommendations submitted via our forms, and non-identifiable usage analytics to improve content curation.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data solely to deliver occasional editorial digests, respond to community contributions, and improve discovery guides across Indian cities. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties.

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or wish to unsubscribe from our newsletter, contact our team at privacy@humaraindia.org.
