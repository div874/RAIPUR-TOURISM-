Travel Information

HOW TO REACH

Air, Train & Highway Access

By Air: Swami Vivekananda Airport (RPR) connects to major Indian metros daily.
By Train: Raipur Junction is a key station on the Mumbai-Howrah main line.
By Road: Connected via National Highways NH-53 and NH-30.

BEST TIME TO VISIT

October to March (Winter)

Pleasant temperatures ranging from 15°C to 28°C ideal for sightseeing. Major festivals like National Tribal Dance Festival and Rajim Kumbh Mela take place in winter.

WHERE TO STAY

Hotels & Homestays

Options range from modern luxury business hotels in Naya Raipur & Telibandha to authentic rural homestays and heritage lodges near Sirpur.

GETTING AROUND

Local Transport

App-based cabs (Ola/Uber), auto-rickshaws, and Raipur City Bus service provide convenient access across both Old Raipur and Naya Raipur corridors.
