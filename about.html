ABOUT HAMARA

A modern city discovery network telling Indian city stories differently.

Our Vision

Hamara is a scalable Indian consumer brand created to uncover the soul of regional cities. Starting with Hamara Raipur, our mission is to build digital city guides that combine editorial journalism, community archives, and AI-powered recommendations.

Why We Exist

Most travel portals reduce vibrant Indian cities into generic booking lists or outdated tourism templates. Hamara Raipur is built for people who want to know a city, not just visit it — connecting travellers and locals with authentic food stalls, living folk traditions, neighborhood histories, and live events.

Future City Expansion

The Hamara discovery design system will expand across Indian cultural capitals — including Hamara Jaipur, Hamara Mumbai, Hamara Pune, and Hamara Nagpur.
